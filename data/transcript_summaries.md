### The Secret Method: Flipping Pharaoh's Sceptres

**Core mechanic:** Buy charged Pharaoh's Sceptres, charge/uncharge them using the Desert Diary perk, and resell at a margin.

### Requirements
- Desert Hard Diary completed (allows charging to 6 charges instead of 3)
- 6 gold artifacts/seals per charge (from Pyramid Plunder — cheap, ~few hundred GP each)
- A Pharaoh's Sceptre to wear (for quick teleport to the Guardian Mummy)
- 200-300M cash stack to cycle multiple sceptres simultaneously
- Patience: slow-buy + slow-sell, NOT insta-buy/sell

### The Loop (per inventory)
1. Teleport to Guardian Mummy with Pharaoh's Sceptre
2. Talk to mummy → spacebar → 1 → spacebar → 1 (charge the sceptres)
3. Each charge takes ~5K in gold artifacts, yields ~130K profit per sceptre (at video time)
4. Each trip takes 20-30 seconds, makes ~520K (4 sceptres)
5. Round-trip: 10 min buying + 30 sec charging = 550K profit
6. Volume: ~8-10 sceptres bought/sold per hour (slow-buy constraint)
7. At peak: 18 per hour theoretical, ~10M+/day cycling multiple inventories

### Price Dynamics
- Were 1-2M before Player-Owned House update → rose to 8M → now ~5M
- Daily price swings of 100-200K per scepter
- Margin exists because: obscure item, low trade volume, niche use (POH teleport nexus + direct teleport)
- Creator found method via Third Age Film's (Soup's) achievement diary guide — Soup did it wrong (insta-buy/sell instead of slow)

### Risk/Warning
- Once public, margins drop fast due to undercutting
- Creator predicts it cycles: margins die → people lose money and quit → margins return in 1-2 months
- Price instability means you can lose margin if scepters drop

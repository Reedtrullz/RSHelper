Hey guys, and welcome to another money making guide.
I have been requested a few times to make a barrows guide, so here it is. Barrows has been one of my favorite money makers for a while now, I now feel reasonably proficient at it.
- 75 Magic
- 75 Range
- 70 Defense
- 43 prayer (70 recommended)
You can get away with a lower range level and a lower defence level. However, I would not do to many chests with under 75 magic.

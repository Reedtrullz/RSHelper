# RSHelper OSRS Event Calendar — Market-Moving Game Updates

Chronological timeline of Grand Exchange economy-shaping events since 2013.
Dates verified against OSRS Wiki API (`{{Update|date=...}}` template).
Events marked `*` are external/market events, not game updates.

---

## 2013 — OSRS Launch Era

| Date | Event | Impact |
|------|-------|--------|
| 22 Feb 2013 | **OSRS Launch** — Early Access opens | Baseline economy begins. No GE yet — all trading is manual (forum/Zybez). Prices extremely volatile. |
| 5 Sep 2013 | **Nightmare Zone** released | NMZ becomes a massive combat XP/gold generator. Long-term effect: inflates combat XP market, introduces herb boxes as daily GP. |
| 17 Oct 2013 | **God Wars Dungeon** released | Godswords, Bandos, Armadyl gear enter the game. Sets the PvM gear hierarchy. Bandos becomes the "default" melee armor for a decade. |

## 2014 — Boss Economy Begins

| Date | Event | Impact |
|------|-------|--------|
| 30 Jan 2014 | **Kraken** released | Trident of the Seas — first powered staff. Sets magic DPS baseline. Kraken tentacle adds item-sink mechanic for Abyssal whip. |
| 18 Sep 2014 | **Bounty Hunter** released | PvP economy gets formalized reward structure. Affects PvP supply items, rune/arrow demand. |
| 16 Oct 2014 | **Corporeal Beast** released | Spirit shields, Arcane/Elysian — BiS defensive offhands. Corp becomes a gold-farming staple. |

## 2015 — Grand Exchange & Zulrah Era

| Date | Event | Impact |
|------|-------|--------|
| 8 Jan 2015 | **Zulrah** — The Solo Snake Boss | **Massive economic impact.** Zulrah's drop table floods the economy with skilling resources (magic logs, sharks, coal, rune ore, pure essence). Causes long-term deflation in skilling supplies. Toxic blowpipe and serpentine helm become PvM staples. Zulrah scales become a currency-like commodity. |
| 26 Feb 2015 | **Grand Exchange** released | The GE arrives. Price discovery becomes centralized. Market efficiency increases dramatically. Arbitrage/manipulation shifts from forum trading to GE flipping. |
| 27 Feb 2015 | **Grand Exchange Tweaks** | Buy limit adjustments, GE slot UI. |
| 1 Apr 2015 | **While Guthix Sleeps & Dragon Claws** | Dragon claws enter OSRS — iconic PvP spec weapon. Affects PvP gear demand, pure pk builds. |
| 6 Jul 2015 | **Additional Grand Exchange Slots** | GE slot cap increased from 6 to 8. Increases flipping capacity. |
| 27 Aug 2015 | **Cerberus** released | Primordial/Pegasian/Eternal crystals — BiS boot upgrades. Creates permanent demand for dragon boots/ranger boots/infinity boots. |
| 1 Oct 2015 | **Abyssal Sire** released | Abyssal dagger, bludgeon. Adds sink for unsired/abyssal items. |
| 29 Oct 2015 | **Deadman Mode** (first season) | DMM economy is separate but drives demand for OSRS bonds (membership). Bond prices affected. |
| 26 Nov 2015 | **Zulrah Scale Fishing** (nerf) | Zulrah drop table adjusted — fewer resources. Skilling resource prices recover partially. |

## 2016 — Zeah & Skilling Economy

| Date | Event | Impact |
|------|-------|--------|
| 7 Jan 2016 | **Zeah: Great Kourend** released | New continent. Lizardman shamans → dragon warhammer. Arceuus RC → blood/soul runes. Hosidius → Tithe Farm. Zeah becomes a major economic zone. |
| 6 May 2016 | **Monkey Madness II** | Demonic gorillas → Zenyte jewelry (Torture, Anguish, Tormented). New BiS jewelry drives demand for onyx. Ballista enters ranged meta. |
| 16 Jun 2016 | **Skotizo** released | Arclight charges, dark totem economy. |
| 8 Sep 2016 | **Wintertodt** released | Firemaking boss. Floods economy with skilling supplies (seeds, herbs, ores, logs, raw fish). Affects ironman economy significantly. Tome of Fire creates fire rune sink. |

## 2017 — Raids 1 & Inferno

| Date | Event | Impact |
|------|-------|--------|
| 5 Jan 2017 | **Chambers of Xeric** (Raids 1) | **Major economic shift.** Twisted bow — defines ranged BiS and drives demand for dragon arrows. Ancestral robes — BiS magic. Dex/Arcane prayer scrolls — universal PvM upgrades. Dragon claws re-enter through CoX. 3rd age becomes "mega-rare" cosmetic status symbol via clue reward mimic. |
| 1 Jun 2017 | **The Inferno** | Infernal cape — BiS melee cape. Creates demand for fire cape sacrifices. Tokkul economy affected. |
| 7 Sep 2017 | **Fossil Island** | Ammonite crabs become the AFK combat meta. Fossil economy. Sulliuscep woodcutting. |
| 26 Oct 2017 | **Grotesque Guardians** | Granite dust, granite hammer. Mid-tier boss economy. |
| 23 Nov 2017 | **Mage Arena II, Revenant Caves** | Revenant weapons (Craw's bow, Viggora's chainmace, Thammaron's sceptre) — BiS wilderness weapons. Revenant ether economy. Affects PvP supply chains. |

## 2018 — Raids 2 & Mobile Launch

| Date | Event | Impact |
|------|-------|--------|
| 4 Jan 2018 | **Dragon Slayer II** | Vorkath — becomes the #1 consistent solo GP/hr boss. Ava's assembler. Dragonfire ward. Superior dragon bones flood prayer market. Vorkath's drop table is extremely resource-heavy — alchables, dragonhide, dragon bones, rune/onyx bolt tips. |
| 7 Jun 2018 | **Theatre of Blood** (Raids 2) | Scythe of Vitur — BiS melee, extremely expensive. Sanguinesti staff. Justiciar armor. Ghrazi rapier. Avernic defender. ToB uniques define melee meta for years. Scythe becomes economy barometer. |
| 26 Jul 2018 | **Revenant Cave Rewards** | Revenant weapons and ether economy adjusted. |
| 6 Aug 2018 | **OSRS Mobile Android Soft Launch** (Canada) | Mobile begins. Player count starts rising. |
| 6 Sep 2018 | **Full Mobile Launch** (Android + iOS) | **Massive player influx.** New casual player demographic. Bond prices affected by membership demand. Skilling supply/demand shifts due to mobile AFK meta. |
| 6 Dec 2018 | **Collection Log** released | Cosmetic/hobbyist content but drives demand for obscure items to "green log." |

## 2019 — Song of the Elves & Twisted League

| Date | Event | Impact |
|------|-------|--------|
| 10 Jan 2019 | **Kebos Lowlands** | Alchemical Hydra — hydra leather (Ferocious gloves), hydra claw (Dragon hunter lance). Konar slayer — brimstone keys add alchable/seed influx. |
| 25 Jul 2019 | **Song of the Elves** | **Major release.** The Gauntlet/Corrupted Gauntlet — high-tier solo PvM with unique rewards (Blade of Saeldor, Crystal armor). Zalcano — skilling boss floods smithing supplies. Prifddinas unlocks new skilling methods. Divine potions. Crystal equipment rework. |
| 26 Sep 2019 | **Fremennik Exiles** | Basilisk jaw → Neitiznot faceguard — BiS melee helm. |
| 14 Nov 2019 | **Twisted League** (Leagues I) | Leagues boost bond demand for alt accounts. |

## 2020 — COVID Surge & Death Rework

| Date | Event | Impact |
|------|-------|--------|
| 6 Feb 2020 | **Nightmare of Ashihama** | Inquisitor armor, Harmonised orb, Eldritch orb, Volatile orb. Nightmare staff. Inquisitor's mace. Introduces crush meta. Extremely rare drops → very high prices. |
| Mar–Apr 2020* | **COVID-19 Lockdown Player Surge** | *External event.* Player count doubles from ~70k to ~150k concurrent. All items spike. Bonds crash then recover. Mass inflation from increased playtime. OSRS becomes a "work from home" background activity. This is a regime change. |
| 4 Jun 2020 | **Darkmeyer** released | Sins of the Father quest. Daeyalt essence for RC. Hallowed Sepulchre — agility GP. Blood shard — BiS amulet slot for sustain. |
| 24 Jun 2020 | **Death Changes** (death cost rework) | Death costs significantly increased for high-value gear. Creates GP/item sink on death. High-end PvM becomes riskier. Death's Coffer introduced as item sink. |
| 2 Jul 2020 | **Poll 71 & Death Feedback Changes** | Death mechanics further tuned. |
| 14 Aug 2020 | **Trailblazer League** (Leagues II) | Bond demand spikes for league alts. |

## 2021 — GE Tax & Combat Achievements

| Date | Event | Impact |
|------|-------|--------|
| 6 Jan 2021 | **Soul Wars** released | Soul cape, ectoplasmator. Zeal tokens economy. |
| 13 Jan 2021 | **God Wars Instancing** | GWD bosses get instances. Increases supply of GWD uniques (Bandos, Armadyl, Zamorak, Saradomin items). Reduces "crash" culture. |
| 16 Jun 2021 | **A Kingdom Divided** | Arceuus spellbook rework — thralls affect PvM meta. Demonic offering — prayer meta shift. |
| 21 Jul 2021 | **Combat Achievements** | Ghommal's hilt — QoL upgrades. Cosmetic prestige drives demand for CA completion items. |
| 22 Sep 2021 | **Wilderness & Equipment Rebalance** | D'hide and dinh's bulwark nerfed in PvP. Affects PvP gear demand. |
| 27 Oct 2021 | **Revenant Caves & Deadman Changes** | Revenant caves reworked. Affects revenant weapon/ether economy. |
| 9 Dec 2021 | **Grand Exchange Tax & Item Sink** | **Major economic change.** 1% GE tax introduced. Taxed GP is used to buy and delete items from the game — creates permanent item sink for high-volume traded items. Torva, Bandos, godswords, etc. begin being removed. Prices stabilize for long-depreciating items. Flipping margins compress (1% friction). |
| 17 Dec 2021 | **Gielinor Gazette — December 2021** | More economy discussion, item sink expansion. |

## Affected Item Category Index

| Category | Key Events | Typical Direction |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| **PvM uniques (weapons)** | CoX, ToB, ToA, Nex, DT2, Araxxor, Scythe buff | New BiS → old BiS depreciates |
| **PvM uniques (armor)** | GWD, Nex (Torva), ToA (Masori), Nightmare (Inquisitor) | New release spikes, then gradual decline |
| **Skilling supplies** | Zulrah, Wintertodt, Vorkath, Guardians of the Rift | Boss drop tables deflate skilling resource prices |
| **Consumables (scales/darts/arrows/runes)** | Zulrah, CoX (dragon arrows), Blowpipe meta | Tied to BiS weapon demand |
| **Bonds** | Mobile launch, COVID, Leagues, Deadman | Player count spikes → bond price spikes |
| **3rd age / mega-rares** | CoX (clue mimic), Collection log | Scarcity + prestige → extreme appreciation |
| **Zenyte jewelry** | MM2 (release), Araxxor (Rancour = Torture sink) | Long-term appreciation as BiS |
| **Nex/ToA component items** | Torva (needs Bandos), Masori (needs Armadyl) | Permanent demand floor for GWD items |
| **GP inflation rate** | GE Tax (1%→2%), Death's Coffer, item sinks | Slows inflation, removes items |
| **Flipping margins** | GE Tax introduction, buy limit changes | Compressed by tax friction |
